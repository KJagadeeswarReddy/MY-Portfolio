In the following sections, I will detail the recommended tech stack, a comprehensive list of features, the design for the AI agent, and a plan for the "daily page" blog.

## Recommended Tech Stack

To build a portfolio that is both powerful and maintainable, I recommend the following technologies:

*   **Framework: [Next.js](https://nextjs.org/)**
    *   **Why:** Next.js is a React framework that offers the best of both worlds: server-side rendering (SSR) for fast initial page loads and search engine optimization (SEO), and static site generation (SSG) for incredible performance. Its file-based routing and API routes make it a breeze to develop with.
*   **Styling: [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/)**
    *   **Why:** Tailwind CSS is a utility-first CSS framework that allows for rapid UI development without writing custom CSS. It's highly customizable and encourages a consistent design system.
*   **Headless CMS: [Sanity.io](https://www.sanity.io/) or [Contentful](https://www.contentful.com/)**
    *   **Why:** A headless CMS will allow you to manage your blog content and project details with ease. It separates your content from your presentation layer, giving you the flexibility to use your content across multiple platforms.
*   **Deployment: [Vercel](https://vercel.com/)**
    *   **Why:** Vercel is the company behind Next.js, and their platform is optimized for deploying Next.js applications. It offers a seamless developer experience with features like automatic deployments, serverless functions, and a global CDN.

## The AI Agent

The AI agent will be a lively and interactive guide for visitors, providing a unique and personalized experience. It will be a friendly chatbot that can answer questions about you, your projects, and your skills.

### Purpose

*   **Engage Visitors:** To create a more engaging and memorable experience for visitors.
*   **Provide Information:** To answer questions and provide information in a conversational way.
*   **Showcase Your Skills:** To demonstrate your ability to work with AI and natural language processing.

### Features

*   **Conversational Interface:** A chat interface where users can ask questions in natural language.
*   **Personalized Responses:** The agent will be trained on your personal data, such as your resume, project descriptions, and blog posts.
*   **Project Recommendations:** The agent can recommend projects based on a visitor's interests.
*   **Contact Information:** The agent can provide your contact information and even help schedule a meeting.

### Tech Stack

*   **Natural Language Processing (NLP):** [Dialogflow](https://cloud.google.com/dialogflow) or [Rasa](https://rasa.com/)
*   **Frontend:** A custom chat component built with React.
*   **Backend:** Serverless functions on Vercel to handle API requests to the NLP service.

## The "Daily Page" Blog

The "daily page" blog, hosted on the `daily.domain.com` subdomain, will be a space for you to share your thoughts, insights, and expertise with the world. It will be a powerful tool for building your personal brand and establishing yourself as a thought leader in your field.

### Why a Subdomain?

*   **Improved SEO:** Hosting the blog on a subdomain can improve your site's SEO by creating a clear separation between your portfolio and your blog content.
*   **Brand Consistency:** A subdomain allows you to maintain a consistent brand across your entire online presence.
*   **Scalability:** A subdomain gives you the flexibility to grow your blog independently of your portfolio.

### Content Strategy

*   **Showcase Your Expertise:** Write about topics you're passionate about and have expertise in. This could include technical tutorials, case studies, or opinions on industry trends.
*   **Tell Your Story:** Share your personal journey, your successes, and your failures. This will help you connect with your audience on a more personal level.
*   **Be Consistent:** Aim to publish new content on a regular basis. This will keep your audience engaged and coming back for more.
